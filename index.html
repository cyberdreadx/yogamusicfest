About TYMF

More Than a Festival

Tulum Yoga Music Fest + Conference Week is a grassroots celebration of the people who make Tulum's conscious community what it is.

For one week, teachers, musicians, healers, artists, studios, hotels, local businesses and community members come together across Tulum — creating more ways to practice, discover, connect and support one another.

Our intention is simple: bring the community together, make wellness more accessible, and create greater visibility for the people and spaces doing this work year-round.

The festival grew from a simple idea — that Tulum's teachers, artists and spaces are stronger when they gather. One week a year, the whole community shares one calendar, one intention, and one celebration.

Whether you come for a single class, one unforgettable festival day, or the full seven-day journey, you become part of it.

The Visionary

Narayani

Founder & Community Weaver

A teacher and organizer rooted in Tulum's yoga, bhakti and wellness community, Narayani has spent years bringing people together through practice, music and ceremony. TYMF grew from that work — a way to give the community one week to celebrate, connect and be seen. She leads the core organizing team alongside teachers, artists and venue partners across Tulum.

Who's Behind It

Iván Baray

Iván Baray, better known as Shivan Dharma, is a visionary entrepreneur, event producer, yogi, medicine musician, singer songwriter, and sound healer. His work weaves together music, consciousness, wellness, and community, creating experiences that inspire connection, transformation, and a deeper relationship with oneself and the world.

Emanuele Righes

Emanuele Righes is a meditation guide, hypnotherapist and community facilitator based in Tulum. As part of the team behind the Tulum Yoga Music Festival, he helps bring together local facilitators, artists, conscious brands and wellness spaces to create meaningful experiences that strengthen connection, creativity and community.

Jayson Barniske

Jayson Barniske is an Ashtanga Yoga teacher, Bhakti practitioner, author and founder of Ashtanga Yoga Tulum. Practicing since 2006 and a longtime student of Tim Miller, he brings traditional Mysore practice, yoga philosophy, meditation and kirtan to an international community. His teaching connects ancient wisdom with everyday life, encouraging discipline, devotion and personal transformation.
